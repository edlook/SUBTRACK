Report a Bug

Please send a bug report by email and include, if you can:

  • The extension version (Settings in the extension, or chrome://extensions)
  • Your Chrome version and operating system
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, with any personal details hidden

Please do not send passwords, card numbers or other sensitive data.
